Questions and answers

On every platform

Does a Mac sync with an iPhone or iPad?
No. Sync between the Mac app and the iPhone and iPad app is not available yet.
How do I get my Pro purchase back?
Use the Apple Account you bought it with and choose Restore Purchases: on iPhone and iPad, in Settings; on the Mac, in Settings > About.
Does Delete All Data also remove what is in iCloud?
No. It deletes the app's data on that device. The synced copy, backups and shared boards are removed from their own screens. On the Mac, turn sync off first.

iPhone and iPad

Does it save what I copy in other apps?
Only while you have background saving running. You start it, a floating window stays visible, and closing it stops it. Without it, the app reads the clipboard only while it is open.
Why do widgets and shortcuts open the app?
Because iOS limits clipboard access when an app is not visible. They open it to copy or save.
Does the keyboard record what I type?
No. It does not request Full Access, has no network features and does not record, store or send what you type.

Mac

Where is the app after I open it?
In the menu bar: click its clipboard icon. Opening it again from Finder shows its main window.
Which permissions does it ask for?
Screen Recording to capture and record. To save without prompts, Paste from Other Apps. Only if you turn on the feature: Microphone (narration), Camera, Input Monitoring (showing keys) and Accessibility (direct paste).
Can it paste for me?
Yes, if you turn on direct paste, which is off by default. Otherwise, choosing an item copies it and you press Command-V.
