History

Copy once. Find it again.

It is off until you turn it on. Then it saves what you copy while the app runs in the menu bar.

  • Text, links, rich text, images and files, with search, even inside images
  • Pick an item and press Command-V, or drag it into another app
  • Optional direct paste, and paste in sequence
  • Pause it, exclude apps or choose how long to keep items

Capture and record

Capture just what you need.

The screen freezes while you select, so open menus stay in the picture.

  • Rectangle, window, full screen or free-form, with a delay
  • Scrolling capture, floating pinned pictures and a screen ruler
  • Screen recording (Pro) with audio, camera, clicks and pressed keys
  • Trim and cut video, change speed, remove sound and export MP4 or GIF

Editor

Mark it up before you share.

Open a snip in the editor and finish it in the same app.

  • Pen, highlighter, arrows, shapes and text
  • Crop, blur, pixelate, backgrounds and combined images
  • Copy text and read QR codes on your Mac
  • Quick Redact covers e-mail addresses, phone numbers, cards and keys. Always check: detection can miss data
  • Copy a download link through your iCloud Drive

Snippets and text

The things you type again and again.

Pin replies, addresses and links with a title and tags.

  • Search your snippets or reach them from the history panel
  • More than 75 text transformations
  • JSON, Base64, hashes and regular expressions
  • Translation with Apple's feature, on macOS 14.4 or later

Shortcuts and sync

Made for your keyboard.

Open the history or start a snip from any app.

  • Change any shortcut, or use the macOS screenshot keys
  • Color picker and optional lock with Touch ID or your password
  • iCloud sync between your Macs (Pro)
  • Backup to iCloud Drive or a file, with optional password (Pro)

History

Everything you copied, sorted your way.

It saves the clipboard when you open the app, or when you tap Paste from Clipboard.

  • Optional background saving: while a small Picture in Picture window is on screen, it saves what you copy in other apps
  • Search, including the text in your images
  • Smart lists that fill themselves from your rules
  • Copy with one tap, delete one by one or clear everything

Snippets and keyboard

Replies ready wherever you type.

Pin replies, addresses and links and organize them with tags.

  • Full keyboard with your history and snippets, no Full Access needed
  • iMessage app to put them in a message
  • Save from other apps with the Share menu

Snip and codes

Text from any image.

Choose a photo, take a picture or paste an image.

  • Crop the part you need and extract its text on your device
  • Keep the crop, the text or both
  • Read QR codes with the camera or from an image

Transforms

Change text without leaving the app.

Touch and hold a text and choose Transform Text.

  • More than 75 transformations
  • Letter case and lines
  • Encoding, hashes and JSON
  • Extraction and cleanup

Sync and share

With you, and with whoever you choose.

Anything that leaves the device is something you turn on.

  • Sync through your private iCloud between your iPhone and iPad (Pro)
  • Shared boards with the people you invite
  • Send straight to a nearby device, once the receiver approves
  • Backup to iCloud Drive or a file, with optional password (Pro)

System and settings

Part of your iPhone.

Reach the app from the places you already use, and set it up your way.

  • Widgets, Live Activity and Control Center controls (iOS 18 or later)
  • Shortcuts, Siri and the Action Button on supported iPhone models
  • Optional lock with Face ID, Touch ID or passcode
  • Light, dark or system appearance

Guides

How to get the most out of it.

Short steps for the things people set up first.

Mac

Turn on the history

Do it in the welcome window or in the app's Settings.

Stop the paste prompt

In System Settings > Privacy & Security > Paste from Other Apps, allow J Clipboard.

Allow screen capture

Give J Clipboard the screen recording permission. In the app, Settings > Permissions shows its status.

Reuse something you copied

Press Control-Command-V, pick an item and press Command-V where you want it, or drag it there.

Copy text from the screen

Press Control-Shift-T and select the area with the text.

Use the macOS screenshot keys

Turn off Apple's versions in Keyboard settings and choose them in the app's Settings > Shortcuts. To go back, use Restore Default Shortcuts and turn Apple's on again.

iPhone and iPad

Stop the paste prompt

In iOS Settings, open J Clipboard and set “Paste from Other Apps” to “Allow”.

Save in the background

In the app, open Settings > Background Saving and start it before you switch to another app.

Add the keyboard

In iOS Settings: General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Add New Keyboard > J Clipboard.

Save from another app

Select something, tap Share, choose J Clipboard, then save it to history or pin it.

Use the Action Button

On supported iPhone models: Settings > Action Button > Shortcut, then pick a J Clipboard action.

Add a Control

On iOS 18 or later: open Control Center, tap Add a Control and choose J Clipboard.
