Privacy Policy

J Clipboard works differently on each platform: read the section for your device, and both if you use both apps. In both, the developer collects no data.

iPhone and iPad · Mac

iPhone and iPad

Effective November 27, 2026

Summary

J Clipboard does not collect any data. The developer has no servers that receive your content, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software.

Data stored on your device

Your clipboard history, pinned snippets, saved images and preferences are stored on your device, in a container shared only between the app and its widgets. They may be included in the device backups you enable with Apple.

Clipboard access

The app reads the clipboard while it is open: when you open or return to it, if automatic saving is on, or when you ask it to paste. It reads it in the background only while you have Background Saving running: you start it yourself, a floating Picture in Picture window stays visible the whole time, and closing that window stops it. The app never saves content marked as concealed or temporary. It also skips text that appears to be a password, key, payment card number or verification code; when you ask to paste such text, you can choose to save it anyway. This detection cannot be perfect, so review your history and delete anything you do not want to keep. Apple's paste button gives the app the content only when you tap it.

Share menu and keyboard

The Share extension saves only what you choose to send to it. The optional keyboard reads your history and pinned snippets to show them and type the one you tap. To suggest and correct words, it checks the text next to the cursor, on your device only, using the iOS spell checker and your iOS text replacements. It does not request Full Access, has no network features and does not record, store or send what you type. The iMessage app shows your history and snippets and puts the one you tap in the message field; you decide whether to send it.

App lock

If you turn on the app lock, iOS verifies you with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. The app only learns whether it succeeded and never receives biometric data. The lock controls what the app, its widgets and its keyboard show; it does not add separate encryption to the data on your device, which is protected by iOS.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo for the Snipping Tool or to scan a QR code. Photos are chosen with the system photo picker, which gives the app only the images you select. If you turn on the screenshot offer, the app asks for full access to Photos and uses it only to find and show your newest screenshot; it saves a copy only if you choose to. Text recognition, including the text used to search your saved images, and code reading run on your device with Apple's Vision framework. A link found in a code opens only if you tap it.

iCloud sync

Sync is off until you turn it on. It keeps your history, pinned snippets, smart lists, saved images and the text found in them on your iPhone and iPad that use the same Apple Account, through the private database of your iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit. The developer cannot read it. Apple stores and protects it under its own privacy policy, and it uses your iCloud storage. The app uses silent notifications from Apple to learn that something changed. Turning sync off keeps your data; Delete Data from iCloud, on the sync screen, removes the synced copy.

Shared boards

A shared board is stored in the iCloud account of the person who created it. Only people invited through Apple's sharing screen can open it, and everything added to a board is visible to all of them, who may also change or delete it, according to the permission they were given. Apple shows participants the names or addresses used for the invitation. The developer cannot see boards or participants. The owner can stop sharing or delete the board; a participant can leave it. Do not add anything you do not want the other members to have.

Nearby transfer

When you open the Nearby screen, your device looks for, or lets itself be found by, other devices running J Clipboard on the local network or by Bluetooth. It shows a name made of your device name and a short random code. The connection is encrypted, goes straight between the two devices, and the receiver must accept each transfer.

Lock Screen

If you turn on the Live Activity, your newest entry can appear on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. With the app lock on, its content is hidden.

Optional backups

If you choose to back up, you can save a file containing your history, snippets and images to your own iCloud Drive, or export it to a location you choose with the Files app. The developer cannot access it. A file in iCloud Drive is protected by Apple and its privacy policy. You can also protect the backup with a password: it is then encrypted on your device. The app does not store the password and cannot recover it.

Purchases

The Pro purchase is processed by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Keeping and deleting data

Data stays until you delete it. You can delete individual entries or clear the history; while sync is on, those deletions also apply to your other synced devices. Settings > Delete All Data erases the data on that device only and turns sync off there. It does not delete backups, the synced copy in iCloud or shared boards: delete a backup from Cloud Backup, the synced copy from the sync screen, and a board from Shared Boards. Deleting the app removes its local data.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be published with a new effective date. For questions, use the developer's support link on the J Clipboard page in the App Store.

Mac

Effective November 27, 2026

Summary

J Clipboard does not collect any data. The developer has no servers that receive your content, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software. The optional iCloud features use your own iCloud account.

Data stored on your Mac

Your clipboard history, pinned snippets, saved images and preferences are stored on your Mac, inside the app's sandbox. They may be included in the backups you make of your Mac, such as Time Machine.

Clipboard history

Clipboard history is off until you turn it on. While it is on and the app is running, it saves what you copy, including copies made in other apps. The menu bar icon shows whether it is saving, and you can pause it, exclude apps or turn it off at any time. It does not save content that its source marks as concealed or temporary, copies made while an excluded app is in front, or, unless you turn this off, text that appears to be a password, key, payment card number or verification code. This detection cannot be perfect, so review your history and delete anything you do not want to keep. macOS may ask you to allow the app to paste from other apps.

Screenshots and screen recording

The app captures your screen only when you ask it to, after you grant the macOS screen recording permission. While recording, a red frame, a Stop button and a red menu bar icon remain visible. The Mac's sound and the microphone are recorded only if you turn those options on. Screenshots and recordings stay on your Mac unless you share or move them. They are saved in a folder only when you choose one or save them yourself.

Camera, clicks and keys in recordings

These options are off until you turn them on. The camera is used only while a recording that shows it is running, and its picture is only part of that video. Clicks are drawn on the video. To show the keys you press, macOS asks for the Input Monitoring permission; the app reads keys only while such a recording is running, only to draw them on screen, and never stores them. macOS does not deliver what you type in password fields. By default only shortcuts and special keys are shown; if you show every key, what you type appears in the video.

Direct paste

Choosing an item copies it. If you turn on direct paste and allow it in System Settings under Accessibility, the app also sends the Command-V keystroke right after you choose what to paste. It sends no other keystroke and does not read or control other apps.

App lock

If you turn on the app lock, macOS verifies you with Touch ID or your password. The app only learns whether it succeeded and never receives biometric data. The lock controls what the app shows; it does not add separate encryption to the data on your Mac.

Text recognition

Text and QR codes are recognized on your Mac with Apple's Vision framework. If search inside images is on, the text found in copied images is stored with your history. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Optional iCloud sync

Sync is off until you turn it on, and is part of Pro. While it is on, your history, snippets and images are stored in your private iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit, so that your Macs signed in to the same Apple Account share them. Sync between the Mac app and the iPhone and iPad app is not offered yet. However, both apps use the same storage area in your private iCloud database: if you turn sync on in both with the same Apple Account, history and snippets from one may appear in the other, and deleting them, or deleting the synced copy, may affect both. The content travels in encrypted fields and the developer cannot read it. Apple handles it under its privacy policy. Deleting an item deletes it from your synced devices. You can remove the synced copy with Settings > Sync > Delete Synced Data From iCloud.

Optional backups

If you choose to back up, the app creates a backup file with your history, snippets and images. You can save it to your own iCloud Drive or export it to a location you choose. The developer cannot access it. A backup in iCloud Drive is handled by Apple under its privacy policy; an exported file is handled by whatever service or device you save it to. You can also protect the backup with a password: it is then encrypted on your Mac before it is saved or sent. The app does not store the password and cannot recover it. The app does not ask you to create an account.

Links and translation

If you choose Copy Link and confirm, the app copies that one picture or recording to your iCloud Drive and asks Apple for a download link. Anyone with the link can download the file until the link expires; to stop sharing it, delete the file from iCloud Drive. Translation uses Apple's translation feature: the text you choose to translate is handled by Apple under its privacy policy.

Purchases

The Pro purchase is processed by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Keeping and deleting data

Data stays until you delete it. You can delete individual entries, clear the history, or use Settings > About > Delete All Data to delete the app's data on your Mac. That does not delete your backups or your synced copy; it turns sync off on this Mac first, so nothing is deleted on your other devices. Delete the iCloud Drive backup from the Backup tab in Settings or from iCloud Drive, and delete exported files wherever you saved them. Deleting the app removes its local data.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be published with a new effective date. For questions, use the developer's support link on the J Clipboard page in the App Store.
